# Scientific contract

Problem discovery supports two paths. COMMUNITY_RECOGNIZED follows a documented important difficulty; it need not invent a new failure or require several papers for a single author-stated limitation. COMMON_FAILURE starts with the application and first principles, links multiple papers, and proposes a shared failure not necessarily named by any author. In both paths challenge premises, construct counterexamples, compare alternative explanations and conflicting findings, reconstruct the strongest existing solution, and distinguish reported evidence, inference and hypotheses. Present one or several valuable candidates with pain, evidence, present solution extent and research value. Do not force novelty by continually narrowing an increment.

Preserve the core application throughout. Refine the question with evidence; material key-question changes require an explicit new human selection. Selected problem versions and route bindings are immutable. Explain what changed and why. Supplementary research resumes the saved scientific object; it does not reset the application or silently select another question.

Choose a concrete technical line that can evolve. Search mature mechanisms in other fields first, understand their assumptions, inputs, outputs and effective boundaries, and use, transfer, modify or fuse them as justified. Design from scratch when evidence supports that choice. Domain identity and actual coverage are separate judgments. Neither global problem/root-cause isomorphism nor modification volume is a contribution criterion. Require a task-relevant, explainable, testable substantive gain; report attribution and limits.

Specify the model, variable meanings/units, inputs, outputs, algorithm operations, mathematical derivations, assumptions and components. Every component needs a purpose, interface, necessity and isolation test. Mark derivations as inferred until checked. Compare alternatives and explain improvement benefit, cost and validation. Optional root-cause analysis or principle search should resolve a concrete uncertainty, not become a ceremonial prerequisite.

Contributions and theory/experiments continuously influence the line. Distinguish design selection from independent contribution validation. Record controls, comparison, metric, setting, falsifying outcome, authorization and result artifacts; evaluate effect size, resource budgets, practical baselines and applicable assumptions. Results must cause revision, gap research or problem reconsideration. In-field strong coverage requires a shared-map update followed by important-pain discovery and a new human choice. Fresh reviewers read originals, steelman prior work and demand evidence; they do not choose for the human. Schema and provenance checks cannot prove scientific truth or top-venue quality.
